How to use FlashPress

1. Make a deck

Click ＋ New deck and give it a name – “Spanish words”, “Biology chapter 3”, “Capitals”. Tell FlashPress what the front and back are (for example Spanish and English) and which language to read aloud.

2. Add cards

On ✏️ Cards click ＋ Add a card and type the front and the back. To add many at once use ☰ More → Add many cards at once: one card per line, front | back, or paste two columns from Excel, Google Sheets or a Quizlet export. With Pro you can put a picture on either side.

3. Study a few minutes every day

On 🧠 Study look at the front, think of the answer, then click Show answer (or press Space). Be honest: Again if you did not know it, Hard, Good or Easy if you did (keys 1–4). FlashPress shows cards you find hard more often and cards you know well less often – each card comes back right before you would forget it. Type the answer checks your spelling, Back first turns the cards around, Read aloud says the words with the voices of Windows.

4. Print real cards

On 🖨 Print choose 8, 10 (business-card size) or 4 cards per page, or index cards 3 × 5 / 4 × 6 (Pro). FlashPress prints a page of fronts and then a page of backs that lines up with it. In the print window choose Print on both sides, Flip on long edge, Scale 100% and Margins: None, then cut along the dashed lines. No two-sided printer? Choose Fold in the middle: print, cut the strips and fold them.

Your data

Everything stays on this PC. Use ☰ More → Save a backup now and then and Open a backup on a new PC. Save this deck as CSV opens in Excel.

Free and Pro

Free: 3 decks with up to 50 cards each, study with smart review, type the answer, read aloud, print 8 / 10 / 4 per page and fold cards, a small “Made with FlashPress” line on printed pages. Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): unlimited decks and cards, pictures on cards, index cards 3 × 5 and 4 × 6, no line on printed pages.
